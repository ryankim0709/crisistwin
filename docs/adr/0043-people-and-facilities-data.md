# 0043: People and facilities data for the scenario engine

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: accepted (data and downloaders; the generator and traffic-model changes
that consume it are not built yet).

Builds on the scenario-engine design in `0030-scenario-engine.md` (residents generator, one
population for traffic and calls) and the traffic parameters in `sim/behavior.py` and `sim/demand.py`.

## Context

The scenario engine needs simulated residents for any California fire. Its current inputs are
thin: `sim/demand.py` takes households and vehicles from ACS counts for one county's block groups,
counts households without a vehicle but never moves them, sends 100% of households in a noticed
block group, and has no notion of age, disability, language, pets, care facilities or schools.
`sim/behavior.py` states that its parameters are assumptions "informed by" research, with no
citations to a table or page. For a drill and for strategy tests we need (a) who lives where and
who cannot self-evacuate, (b) which facilities hold people who cannot, and (c) behaviour numbers a
reader can check.

## Decision

1. **Block-group vulnerability counts** from the ACS 5-year (Census Bureau, public domain): B25044
   (households by vehicles), B01001 (age, for 65 and over), B18101 (disability), C16002 (limited
   English households). `tools/people_data.py census` pulls them for a county list or bbox and
   records URL, bytes and SHA-256 of every response. The Census Data API is the default and runs
   without a key at low volume; `CENSUS_API_KEY` is optional. The bulk summary files at
   `www2.census.gov` are a keyless fallback. Block-group internal points come from the TIGER/Line
   file. B18101 is not published below tract, so each block group carries its tract's counts and the
   extract says so.
2. **Facilities** from state registries: CDPH licensed and certified facilities (hospitals, skilled
   nursing, intermediate care, adult day health), CDSS Community Care Licensing (residential care
   for the elderly, adult residential, adult day care, child care centres), and CDE school sites.
   `tools/people_data.py facilities` records the licence statement of each. Capacity is the
   published licensed capacity; for schools it is enrolment (a proxy).
3. **Behaviour parameters** live in `tools/people_behavior.json`, each with `status` (sourced,
   derived, assumption, gap), source id, document locator and, where `sim/behavior.py` or
   `sim/demand.py` has a counterpart, the current value and a note on how it differs. A test checks
   the file against the live constants, so drift is caught.
4. **De-identification.** CDSS homes of six beds are private houses, so CDSS rows carry no name,
   street, phone or licensee and their points are rounded to 3 decimals (about 100 m). CDPH contacts
   and CDE administrators are never read. Hospitals, nursing facilities and schools are public
   institutions and keep their names.
5. **Fixtures.** One Butte County extract per dataset is committed (`tools/people_acs_butte_fixture.json`,
   105 KB; `tools/people_facilities_butte_fixture.json`, 90 KB), labelled `evidence_class: observed`
   and generated by the tool. Tests use only these and injected fake fetchers; the network is never used.

## Consequences

- The residents generator can draw carless, older, disabled and limited-English households by block
  group, and place facilities with capacities. The traffic model can add a carless pickup demand and
  a shadow-evacuation demand, and replace `EVACUATION_PARTICIPATION = 1.0` with a sourced range.
  `docs/people-data.md` lists the exact uses and the open gaps.
- Observed data and assumptions stay apart: every behaviour number is tagged, and the three
  placeholders we added (order-compliance default 0.92, shadow share 0.30, pet delay 10 min) are
  marked `assumption`.
- **Vintage mismatch.** The ACS fixture is 2018-2022 on 2020 block groups. Paradise lost most of its
  population after November 2018, so the fixture does not describe Camp-day demography, and its
  geoids differ from the 2018 TIGER block groups in the Camp package. A Camp-day extract needs ACS
  2014-2018 (API only, so a key) joined to the 2018 geometry.
- **Facility roster is today's.** The registries list facilities open in 2026, not those open in
  2018. Feather River Hospital and the Paradise care homes are not in the fixture. NIST TN 2252 gives
  their 2018 evacuation facts (60 patients, Cypress Meadows 91 patients) as scenario examples, not as
  a roster.
- **Census keyless access did not work from the build environment.** The Data API answered every
  data request with its "Missing Key" page on 2026-10-24 (metadata calls worked). The downloader
  detects this and stops with a clear message. The fixture was therefore built with `--source bulk`.
  The API path is unit-tested on canned responses but was not run end to end here.
- Licences: Census public domain; CDPH and CDSS state "Terms of Use" with no open licence; CDE states
  none. The fixtures keep those statements. If the repository is ever published, confirm CDE and the
  state portal terms first.

## Alternatives considered

- Census API only. Rejected as the sole path: it failed keyless in the build environment.
- CDE `pubschls.txt` directory download. Rejected: it sits behind a bot wall that blocked repeat
  downloads; the CDE school-site layer on data.ca.gov has coordinates and enrolment and no wall.
- Fitting delay distributions from the survey papers. Not possible: they report clock hour or day,
  not minutes after a message (see the gaps in `docs/people-data.md`).
